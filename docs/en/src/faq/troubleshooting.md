# FAQ and troubleshooting

Status: draft

`wpg doctor` checks permissions, Npcap, setcap, drivers and hugepages, and prints OS-specific fix commands. This page will map each doctor finding to its cause and remedy, for example a missing CAP_NET_RAW on Linux or a missing Npcap on Windows. It also answers common questions such as why the rate falls short of the target. Entries are added as issues are reported.
